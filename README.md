## / THE IDEA

Progress doesn't come from a single productive day. It comes from showing up consistently.

**Habit Tracker** is a web application built around that idea. It provides a digital space to organise daily habits, record progress, and develop a more consistent routine.

The objective is straightforward: make tracking simple enough to maintain and progress visible enough to motivate.

## / FEATURES

**Habit Management**
Organise daily routines and keep your habits in one place.

**Progress Tracking**
Record your progress and maintain a clearer picture of your consistency.

**Simple Interface**
